## Original assets (created for this project)
Everything else is original and generated by scripts in this repository — no stock, ripped or AI-scraped assets:
* **Vehicles:** procedural vector art from `src/gameplay/vehicle/vehicle_shape.gd` + `data/vehicles.json` (61 original designs and names).
* **Scenery & props:** `src/gameplay/world/decor_painter.gd`, `backdrop.gd`, `terrain_chunk.gd`.
* **Icons:** `tools/build_icons.py` → `assets/icons/*.svg`.
* **Textures, app icon, splash, feature graphic:** `tools/build_textures.py`.
* **Audio:** engine loops, sound effects and three music tracks synthesised by `tools/build_audio.py` (numpy + ffmpeg/libvorbis for encoding).

## Trademarks
Vehicle names and makers in the game are fictional. Real models are referenced only internally (`inspired_by` in `data/vehicles.json`) as design notes; they are never shown to players, and no manufacturer logos or trade dress are used. Real branding may be enabled per vehicle only after obtaining a licence (see ARCHITECTURE.md §8).
